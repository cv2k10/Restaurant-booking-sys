Our Story

Crafted with
Passion & Purpose

Founded in 2009 by Chef Antoine Dubois, Maison Dorée was born from a singular conviction: that dining is not merely sustenance, but one of life's most profound pleasures. Nestled in the heart of Manhattan, our restaurant has become a sanctuary for those who seek culinary excellence.

Every dish on our menu is a love letter to classical French technique, reimagined with contemporary sensibility and the finest seasonal ingredients sourced from trusted farms and artisan producers around the world.

Our sommelier team curates a cellar of over 800 labels, ensuring the perfect pairing for every occasion — from an intimate dinner for two to a milestone celebration with friends.
